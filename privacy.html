English

Introduction

Pixel Weather ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

1. Location Data

  • We collect your device's location to provide accurate weather information
  • Location data is used only to fetch weather forecasts and is not stored permanently
  • You can grant or revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings

2. User Preferences

  • Temperature unit preference (Celsius/Fahrenheit)
  • App theme preference (Dark/Light/System)
  • Language preference
  • Favorite locations (stored locally and encrypted)

3. Device Information

  • Device model and operating system version (for app compatibility)
  • App version and crash reports (to improve stability)

How We Use Your Information

  • Weather Services: Your location is used solely to provide weather forecasts
  • App Functionality: Preferences are stored to personalize your experience
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage data helps us improve the app
  • Security: We perform security checks to protect against tampering and ensure app integrity

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

1. Weather Data Provider

  • We use weather APIs to fetch forecast data
  • Weather provider may collect location data according to their privacy policy

2. RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

  • Used for premium subscription features
  • Processes payment information (we do not store payment details)
  • Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

3. Google AdMob (Advertisements)

  • Shows ads to non-premium users
  • May collect device identifiers and usage data
  • Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Google Firebase

  • Used for crash analytics and app performance monitoring
  • Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: Preferences and favorite locations are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Security Measures: We implement security checks to prevent unauthorized access and tampering

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all data stored in the app through Settings
  • Deletion: You can clear all app data through device settings or by uninstalling the app
  • Opt-Out: You can disable location services or upgrade to Premium to remove ads

Children's Privacy

Pixel Weather does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the app and on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us:

  • Email: support@aralbenli.com
  • GitHub: https://github.com/AralBenli
