Common questions

The widget shows "location unavailable".

Open the app once and allow location access ("While Using the App"), then the widget refreshes on its own.

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Settings > Premium > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account (or Google account on Android).

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Pixel Weather.
Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.

Where does the weather data come from?

AccuWeather.
